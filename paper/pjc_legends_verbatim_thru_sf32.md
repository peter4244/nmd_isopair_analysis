# PJC legends verbatim (SF24 through SF32)

Extracted paragraphs 1-13 of the PJC document.

SF24 | Isopair Splice Event Categories. Schematic of the twelve splice-event types identified when comparing a paired isoform (Comparator, bottom track) to the gene's dominant non-NMD isoform (Reference, top track). Grey boxes, exons; horizontal lines with chevrons, introns (chevrons point in the direction of transcription, 5′ → 3′). Categories: transcript-end variants (Alt TSS, Alt TES); alternative splice sites (A5SS, A3SS); exon skipping (Skipped Exon, Missing Internal); and intron retention in six forms (Partial IR 5′, Partial IR 3′, Full IR, IR diff 5′, IR diff 3′, IR diff 5′ + 3′).

SF25 | Isoforms per Gene in Isopair Analysis. Distribution of the number of expressed isoforms per gene across the 3,009 genes for which gene-matched NMD-susceptible / reference / non-NMD isoform triplets could be constructed. The median gene contributes 7 isoforms (dashed line). Counts include all long-read isoforms passing the expression filter in at least one of the four cell types (AT2, LAE, FB, MV) under DMSO.

SF26 | Reference Share of Gene Expression. Distribution across 3,009 genes (each contributing a gene-matched NMD-susceptible / reference / non-NMD isoform triplet) of the reference isoform's DMSO 4-cell-type mean expression as a fraction of the parent gene's total non-NMD expression. Reference isoforms are the highest-expressed non-NMD isoform per gene under DMSO across AT2, LAE, FB and MV. Dashed line, median; dotted line, 50% threshold.

SF27 | Transcript Length by Isopair Analysis Category. Violin plot of transcript length (log10 nt) for the 3,009 gene-matched isoform triplets: NMD comparator (orange), Reference (grey), Control comparator (blue). Reference is the highest-expressed non-NMD isoform per gene under DMSO across the four cell types (AT2, LAE, FB, MV); the NMD and Control comparators are the paired NMD-susceptible and non-NMD non-reference isoforms from the same gene. Solid black bar, median; dotted bars, 25th and 75th percentiles.

SF28 | Isopair analysis flowchart. Data flow for the isoform pairs analyses. Every filter step shows the number of pairs kept and dropped, and a one-line description of why each dropout happened.

SF29 | Splice Events Where NMD Susceptible Isoform Gains Sequence Content. Percentage of detected splice events (across 3,009 gene-matched isoform triplets, both NMD and Control comparators) in which the comparator has additional sequence relative to its reference isoform (GAIN direction). One bar-pair per event type — NMD comparators (orange) vs Control comparators (blue) — sorted by NMD-side %GAIN, descending. Dashed line, 50% (no directional preference). Direction is defined from the comparator's perspective (see SF24 for event-type definitions). Significance stars, Fisher's exact test on the 2 × 2 (GAIN/LOSS × NMD/Control) count table per event type.

SF30 | NMD-Response Magnitude vs Stop-Codon Distance to Last EJC. Per-comparator isoform NMD-response magnitude (mashr posterior-mean log₂ fold-change under SMG1 inhibition, averaged across AT2, LAE, FB and MV) plotted against the distance from the comparator's stop codon to the transcript's last exon-junction complex. Positive values, stop codon upstream of the last EJC (candidate NMD substrate); negative values, stop codon downstream of the last EJC. EJC, exon junction complex; grey points, individual comparators (1st–99th percentile of x-axis shown); orange line, local-linear smoother; orange band, bootstrap 95% CI. Dashed red line, 50-nt PTC threshold. Population: n = 1,166 comparators (all of Subset 2, gene-matched ref-AUG-traceable). Stop anchor: reference-AUG-traced (the first in-frame stop reached after projecting the reference gene's AUG onto the comparator); this avoids the attenuation that would arise from TD2 anchoring on non-PTC main ORFs. Median log₂ fold-change is 2.20 above the 50-nt threshold and 1.09 below it (Wilcoxon one-sided p = 1.1 × 10⁻²⁰).

SF31 | NMD Effect Size by Downstream EJC Count in PTC-Positive NMD Comparators. Distribution of NMD-response magnitude (mashr posterior-mean log₂ fold-change under SMG1 inhibition, averaged across AT2, LAE, FB and MV) as a function of the number of EJCs downstream of the comparator's stop codon. Restricted to comparators with at least one downstream EJC (n = 1,050), drawn from the same Subset 2 population as SF30. Downstream-EJC counts of 7 or more are collapsed into a single "7+" bin. Violin, kernel density; overlaid box, interquartile range and median; red line, per-bin median trend. Per-bin sample counts are shown above each violin. Stop anchor: reference-AUG-traced, matching SF30. EJC, exon junction complex.

SF32 + SF35 | Coding-sequence length and two complementary 3′UTR-length measures, at two cohort scopes. Row 1 (n = 190 GENCODE-restricted) supports SF32; row 2 (n = 1,166 ref-AUG-traceable) supports SF35.

The figure presents the same three measures (coding-sequence length; 3′UTR length under a naïve translation-based measure; 3′UTR length under a bias-corrected measure that walks past any premature stop codon) at two complementary cohorts. The top row uses the strict annotated cohort in which all three transcripts of every triplet are annotated coding transcripts (n = 72 NMD with a premature termination codon; n = 118 NMD without; n = 190 Control; the cohort that supports Figure 3 Panels D–F and Figure 4 Panels A and B). The bottom row uses the broader cohort in which the reference transcript is annotated and the reference start codon can be projected onto each comparator (n = 1,050 NMD with a premature termination codon; n = 116 NMD without; n = 1,166 Control; the cohort that supports Figure 4 Panels C and D). Premature-termination-codon classification in the top row is taken from each comparator's own annotated stop codon under the canonical 50-nt rule; in the bottom row it is taken from the reference start codon projected onto the comparator transcript, which avoids the systematic premature-termination-codon-avoidance bias of the TransDecoder2 CDS caller (documented in SF33/SF34).

(A) Coding-sequence length is similar across the three groups in the annotated cohort (medians 760 / 834 / 968 nt). NMD-susceptible isoforms with a premature termination codon have a modestly shorter coding sequence than Controls (Hodges–Lehmann shift = −186 nt, p = 5×10⁻³), consistent with the premature termination codon truncating the open reading frame. Coding-sequence length is not a confound for the 5′UTR and 3′UTR differences shown in the other panels.

(B) 3′UTR length under the translation-based measure (transcript length minus annotated stop position). NMD-susceptible isoforms with a premature termination codon appear to have substantially longer 3′UTRs than Controls (medians 869 vs 517 nt; p = 1×10⁻⁴), but this is a measurement artifact: the post-stop region in these isoforms includes the coding sequence between the premature stop and the natural stop.

(C) 3′UTR length under the bias-corrected measure that walks past any premature stop codon (translates downstream from the annotated start, skipping stops that would themselves be classified as premature, until reaching the first non-premature stop). Under this corrected measure, NMD with a premature termination codon and Controls are indistinguishable (medians 524 vs 517 nt; p = 0.90), confirming that the apparent inflation in Panel B was the measurement, not the biology. NMD-susceptible isoforms without a premature termination codon retain a significantly shorter 3′UTR than Controls (median 384 vs 517 nt; p = 0.03). Six of the 72 NMD-with-PTC isoforms (8%) had no non-premature stop downstream in the comparator transcript and are dropped from this panel (n = 66 displayed).
