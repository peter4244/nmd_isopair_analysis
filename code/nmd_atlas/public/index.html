    Welcome

    This atlas accompanies Leshem et al. "Long-read characterization of nonsense-mediated decay across primary human lung cell types" (2026). Search a gene above to view its isoforms, transcript structures, expression, and NMD response under SMG1 inhibition (treatment that stabilises NMD substrates) across alveolar type 2 (AT), large airway epithelial (DD), fibroblast (FB), and microvascular endothelial (MV) cells.

    Featured genes: SRSF8, ATF4, SRSF3, SRSF7, CFTR, SMG1.

    Source code & data: github.com/peter4244/nmd_isopair_analysis · Manuscript: Leshem et al. 2026
