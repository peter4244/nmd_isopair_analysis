**Figure 5 | A deep-learning model predicts NMD susceptibility from sequence and learns canonical start-codon and stop-codon context.**

**(A)** Model architecture. For each transcript a candidate-open-reading-frame selector picks five open reading frames; the selector prefers the annotated coding sequence first, then the standard caller's choice, then the open reading frames with the strongest Kozak start context. Each candidate is encoded as a 9-channel × 500-nucleotide window centred on its start codon and an analogous window centred on its stop codon, together with five structural features (start and stop positions as fractions of transcript length, whether the open reading frame matches the annotated or caller-selected coding sequence, and the count of exon–exon junctions downstream of the stop). Shared-weight convolutional encoders process the start and stop windows; a linear encoder processes the structural features. The three per-candidate encodings are concatenated into a 64-dimensional embedding. A learned softmax attention aggregates the five per-candidate embeddings into a single per-transcript embedding that feeds a classifier returning the NMD prediction. Bracketed labels indicate where each interpretability method operates.

**(B)** Receiver-operating-characteristic curve on a held-out test set (transcripts from a fixed subset of chromosomes excluded from training; paralogous gene families held out together). AUC = 0.93, AUPRC = 0.83. Diagonal = chance. Precision–recall curve is inset.

**(C)** Branch-level Shapley attribution at the branch-aggregation point of the trained model, pooled across NMD-susceptible isoforms in the held-out test set. The structural features carry 60.7% of the model's NMD-call signal; the stop-codon window carries 28.8%; the start-codon window carries 10.5%.

**(D)** Mean absolute Shapley attribution per structural feature (pooled across replicate runs). The count of exon–exon junctions downstream of the stop dominates (mean |SHAP| = 2.153), approximately 15× the next-largest feature.

**(E)** Per-nucleotide signed Shapley attribution × input around the start codon, for NMD-susceptible isoforms. Letter height represents mean signed attribution; positive (above the zero line) pushes the prediction toward NMD. The canonical Kozak preferences (G or A at position −3, C at position −1, G at position +4) are recovered as the dominant positive letters at those positions.

**(F)** Same construction as Panel E around the stop codon.

**(G)** Fraction of the model's attention directed to candidate upstream open reading frames per isoform, evaluated on the 1,166-pair ref-AUG-traceable cohort (the same cohort as Figure 4 Panels C and D), stratified by isoform subclass. A candidate open reading frame was counted as a credible upstream open reading frame if its length was below 200 nucleotides (below the 270-nt floor of annotated coding sequences, so the rule cannot capture a real annotated coding sequence), if it both started and terminated within the 5′ half of the transcript, and if it had a credible Kozak start context. The rule depends only on the candidate open reading frame's own structure and does not anchor on the standard caller's choice of main coding sequence, so it is not affected by that caller's premature-termination-codon-avoidance bias (Figure 3 and the TD2 Bias Evidence supplement). 43% of NMD-susceptible isoforms without a premature termination codon (n = 95) place more than 5% of model attention on a candidate upstream open reading frame, compared to essentially zero in the NMD-with-PTC group (n = 1,016) and the Control group (n = 1,107).

Stars: ****p < 10⁻¹⁰, ***p < 10⁻⁴, **p < 10⁻³, *p < 0.05, n.s. otherwise (Mann–Whitney U).

**Abbreviations.** NMD, nonsense-mediated decay; PTC, premature termination codon; ORF, open reading frame; UTR, untranslated region; AUC, area under the receiver-operating-characteristic curve; AUPRC, area under the precision–recall curve; SHAP, Shapley additive explanation; Kozak, the consensus sequence around the translation initiation codon (Kozak 1987).
