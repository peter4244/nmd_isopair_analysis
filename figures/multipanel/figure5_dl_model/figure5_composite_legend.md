**Figure 5 | A deep-learning model predicts NMD susceptibility from sequence and learns canonical start- and stop-codon context.**

**(A)** Model architecture. Per transcript, the ORFik scan and a priority selector pick five candidate ORFs (reference CDS > TD2 CDS > top-Kozak); each ORF is encoded as a 9-channel × 500-nt window around its AUG and its stop codon plus 5 structural features. Shared-weight CNN branches encode AUG and stop windows; a linear branch encodes structural features; concatenation and fusion yield a 64-dim per-ORF embedding. Softmax attention aggregates the five ORF embeddings into a single transcript embedding that feeds a classification head returning the NMD logit. Bracketed labels indicate where each interpretability method operates: Joint DeepSHAP at the input layer, KernelSHAP at the fusion layer, attention weights at the aggregator. **(B)** Held-out performance on the chr-1/3/5/7 paralog-free test set. AUC = 0.93, AUPRC = 0.83. Diagonal = chance; PR-curve inset (lower-right). **(C)** KernelSHAP branch decomposition at the fusion layer: Structural 60.7%, Stop 28.8%, AUG 10.5% of the total NMD-call signal. **(D)** Mean |SHAP| per structural feature (5 DeepSHAP replicates pooled). Downstream EJC count dominates (2.153), ~15× the next feature. **(E)** Per-nucleotide signed SHAP × input around the start codon, NMD class. Letter height = mean signed attribution; positive (above zero) = pushes prediction toward NMD. Canonical Kozak preferences (G/A at −3, C at −1, G at +4) are recovered as the dominant positive letters at those positions. **(F)** Same construction for the stop codon. **(G)** Fraction of the model's attention directed to candidate upstream ORFs (uORFs) per isoform, on the n = 1,166 ref-AUG-traceable subset of gene-matched NMD vs Control pairs (the same scope as Fig 4 C/D). The model distributes attention across five candidate ORFs per transcript; we counted a candidate ORF as a uORF if its length was <200 nt (below the 270-nt floor of GENCODE-annotated CDSes, so the rule cannot capture a real annotated CDS), if it started and terminated within the 5′ half of the transcript, and if it had a credible Kozak start (kozak score ≥ 1). The rule depends only on the candidate ORF's own structure — it does not anchor on any CDS caller's choice of which ORF is the main CDS, so it is not affected by TD2's known PTC-avoidance bias (Fig 3). 43% of NMD+/PTC− isoforms (n = 95) place >5% of model attention on a candidate uORF; PTC+ (n = 1,016) and Control (n = 1,107) are essentially zero. Stars: ****p < 10⁻¹⁰, ***p < 10⁻⁴, **p < 10⁻³, *p < 0.05, n.s. otherwise (Mann–Whitney U).

**Abbreviations.** NMD, nonsense-mediated decay; PTC, premature termination codon; ORF, open reading frame; AUG, methionine start codon; UTR, untranslated region; EJC, exon–exon junction complex; CDS, coding sequence; TD2, TransDecoder2; CNN, convolutional neural network; AUC, area under the ROC curve; AUPRC, area under the precision–recall curve; SHAP, SHapley Additive exPlanation; Kozak PWM, canonical Kozak position weight matrix.
