**Figure 3. Isopair gene-matched isoform-pair analysis identifies skipped exons and premature termination codons as the dominant drivers of NMD susceptibility.**

**(A)** Isopair construction. Per gene, the highest-expressed non-NMD isoform serves as the reference; an NMD pair contrasts it with an SMG1i-responsive comparator and a Control pair with the next-most-expressed non-NMD comparator. Drawn from 3,009 NMD and 3,009 Control pairs across 3,099 eligible genes. **(B)** Density of exonic sequence shared between comparator and reference (`pct_shared`); NMD comparators share more sequence than Controls, consistent with NMD arising from targeted splicing. **(C)** Per-event-type prevalence; SE is roughly twice as prevalent in NMD pairs (44.2 vs 21.2%, p ≈ 10⁻⁸¹). **(D)** Distance from each comparator's stop codon to its last exon–exon junction under reference-AUG-traced ORFs; positive = PTC direction. Direct PTC rate **83.5% NMD vs 16.3% Control** (Fisher OR ≈ 5.1; n = 2,289 NMD, 1,763 Control). **(E)** Proportion of each event type among Isopair-attributed PTC-causing events in NMD (coral) vs all events in Controls (light blue); SE accounts for 52.8% of PTC-causing events vs 8.9% of Control events. **(F)** PTC-causing events stratified by mechanism: frameshift (red), in-frame stop (blue), 3' UTR splice (teal); skipped exons dominate with frameshift the leading mechanism. Stars: ***p < 0.001, **p < 0.01, *p < 0.05, ns, non-significant (Fisher's exact, per event).

**Abbreviations.** NMD, nonsense-mediated decay; PTC, premature termination codon; EJC, exon–exon junction complex; ORF, open reading frame; AUG, start codon; UTR, untranslated region; SMG1i, SMG1 kinase inhibitor; SE, skipped exon; A5SS / A3SS, alternative 5′ / 3′ splice site; IR, intron retention; Partial IR 5′/3′, partial intron retention at the 5′/3′ end; IR differs, intron retained in both with different boundaries; Missing internal, internal exon present in reference but absent in comparator; Alt TSS / TES, alternative transcription start / end site; TD2, TransDecoder2.
