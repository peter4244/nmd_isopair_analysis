**Figure 4. Two orthogonal NMD mechanisms — a canonical premature termination codon plus downstream junction, and an elevated burden of upstream open reading frames — together account for nearly all NMD susceptible isoforms across two complementary cohorts.**

**(A)** 5′UTR length in the strict annotated cohort (n = 48 NMD with a premature termination codon, 82 NMD without, 130 Control). Each comparator is classified using its own annotated stop codon under the canonical 50-nucleotide rule; no projection from the reference is used. NMD susceptible isoforms without a premature termination codon have 5′UTRs approximately three times longer than both the NMD-with-PTC group and the Control group (both pairwise p ≤ 3×10⁻¹²). Violin width is per-group density; horizontal lines mark q25 (dotted), median (dashed), and q75 (dotted).

**(B)** Longest open reading frame within the 5′UTR on the same cohort. NMD susceptible isoforms without a premature termination codon carry a substantial 5′UTR open reading frame (median 224 nucleotides), whereas the NMD-with-PTC group and the Control group are dominated by a peak at zero.

**(C)** 5′UTR length in the broader cohort in which the reference transcript is annotated and the reference start codon can be projected onto each comparator (756 NMD with a premature termination codon, 63 NMD without, 819 Control; the two arms are matched on gene and reference, so each gene contributes one NMD pair and one Control pair). Of 819 NMD-comparator pairs in which the reference start codon could be projected through the comparator's spliced sequence and a matched Control was available at the same gene and reference, 756 (92%) had a stop codon followed by a downstream junction (NMD with a premature termination codon) and 63 did not (NMD without). The same NMD-with-PTC / NMD-without-PTC dichotomy as Panel A holds at this ~6× larger sample.

**(D)** Longest 5′UTR open reading frame in the broader cohort. NMD susceptible isoforms without a premature termination codon carry longer 5′UTR open reading frames than both other groups; the NMD-with-PTC vs Control contrast also reaches significance at this larger sample size.

Stars: ****p < 10⁻¹⁰, ***p < 10⁻⁴, **p < 10⁻³, *p < 0.05, n.s. otherwise (Wilcoxon rank-sum with Hodges–Lehmann shift estimate).
