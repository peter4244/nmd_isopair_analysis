**Figure 4. Two orthogonal NMD mechanisms — canonical PTC plus downstream EJC, and elevated 5′UTR ORF burden — together account for nearly all NMD-responsive isoforms across two complementary analytical scopes.**

**(A)** 5′UTR length, all-3-ENST + coding-CDS scope (n = 72 NMD+/PTC+, 118 NMD+/PTC−, 190 Control). PTC status determined from each comparator's own GENCODE-annotated stop codon (50-nt rule) — no projection from the reference. NMD+/PTC− isoforms have 5′UTRs ~3× longer than both PTC+ and Control (both pairwise p ≤ 1×10⁻¹⁷). Violin width is per-group density; horizontal lines mark q25 (dotted), median (dashed), q75 (dotted). **(B)** Longest 5′UTR ORF at the same scope; NMD+/PTC− carries a substantial 5′UTR ORF (median 224 nt) whereas PTC+ and Control are dominated by a zero-spike. **(C)** 5′UTR length, ENST-reference + ref-AUG-traceable scope, 1:1 gene-matched (re-intersected on `(gene_id, reference_isoform_id)` after the ref-AUG-traceable category filter so NMD and Control panels share the same gene set).
