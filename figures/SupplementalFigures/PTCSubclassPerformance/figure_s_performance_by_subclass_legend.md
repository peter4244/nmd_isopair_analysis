**Supplemental Figure | Deep-learning model discrimination is lower in NMD-susceptible isoforms without a premature termination codon.**

Held-out test-set performance of the trained NMD predictor, restricted to isoforms in the 1,166-pair ref-AUG-traceable cohort (the same cohort that supports Figure 4 Panels C and D and Figure 5 Panel G) and stratified by the same three subclasses: NMD-susceptible with a premature termination codon (NMD+/PTC+, n = 255), NMD-susceptible without a premature termination codon (NMD+/PTC−, n = 30), and matched non-NMD controls (n = 276). Subgroup sample sizes reflect held-out-set membership rather than total cohort.

**(A)** Receiver-operating-characteristic curves for the two NMD-vs-Control contrasts, using the model's predicted NMD probability as the score. The NMD+/PTC+ vs Control contrast achieves AUC = 0.96 (AUPRC = 0.94). The NMD+/PTC− vs Control contrast achieves AUC = 0.74 (AUPRC = 0.26). Diagonal = chance. The substantial AUC drop indicates that NMD-susceptible isoforms without an annotated premature termination codon are intrinsically harder for the model to discriminate from non-NMD isoforms — consistent with the loss of structural and stop-window signal documented in the companion supplemental figure on branch-level attribution.

**(B)** Distribution of the per-isoform predicted NMD probability by subclass. NMD+/PTC+ isoforms cluster at high probability (mean = 0.85), comfortably above the 0.5 decision threshold; non-NMD controls cluster at low probability (mean = 0.19); NMD+/PTC− isoforms straddle the threshold (mean = 0.39), placing many of them on the Control side of the decision boundary. Boxplots show median, IQR, and 1.5×IQR whiskers; the dashed horizontal line marks the 0.5 default decision threshold.

**Abbreviations.** AUC, area under the receiver-operating-characteristic curve; AUPRC, area under the precision–recall curve; NMD, nonsense-mediated decay; PTC, premature termination codon; IQR, interquartile range.
