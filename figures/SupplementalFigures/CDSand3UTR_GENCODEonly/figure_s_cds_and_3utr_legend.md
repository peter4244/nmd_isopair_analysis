**Figure S. CDS length and two complementary 3′UTR-length measures at two cohort scopes — Section A (n = 72/118/190, all-3-ENST + coding-CDS) and Section C (n = 1,050/116/1,166, ENST-reference + ref-AUG-traceable re-intersected on (gene_id, reference_isoform_id)).**

**Row 1 — Section A (own-GENCODE-stop classifier; n = 72/118/190).** Stage-2 gene-matched isoform pairs in which the reference, NMD comparator, and Control comparator are all GENCODE-annotated coding ENST transcripts; PTC classification from each comparator's own GENCODE-annotated stop codon and the 50-nt rule (same isoform set as Figure 3 Panels D/E/F and Figure 4 Panels A/B). **(A)** CDS length is similar across the three groups (medians 760 vs 834 vs 968 nt); NMD+/PTC+ has a modestly shorter CDS than Control (HL shift = −186 nt, p = 5 × 10⁻³), consistent with the canonical PTC truncating the open reading frame. CDS length is not a confound for the 5′UTR and 3′UTR differences shown in the other panels. **(B)** 3′UTR length, **translation-based** (tx_length(comparator) − own_GENCODE_stop_tx_position). NMD+/PTC+ isoforms appear to have substantially **longer** 3′UTRs than Controls (medians 869 vs 517 nt; p = 1 × 10⁻⁴), but this is a **measurement artifact**: the post-stop region in PTC+ pairs includes the coding sequence between the PTC and the natural stop. **(C)** 3′UTR length, **non-PTC-stop based** (walk in-frame downstream from the comparator's own GENCODE ATG, skipping any stop that has a downstream EJC > 50 nt, until reaching the first non-PTC stop; then tx_length − that_position). Under this bias-corrected measure, NMD+/PTC+ and Controls are **indistinguishable** (medians 524 vs 517 nt; p = 0.90), confirming Panel B's PTC+ inflation was the measure, not biology. NMD+/PTC− isoforms retain a **significantly shorter** 3′UTR than Controls (median 384 vs 517 nt; p = 0.03). Six of the 72 NMD+/PTC+ pairs (8 %) had no non-PTC stop downstream in the comparator transcript and are dropped from Panel C (n = 66 displayed).

**Row 2 — Section C (ref-AUG-projected classifier; n = 1,050/116/1,166).** ENST-reference pairs with ref-AUG tracing (categories *effectively_ptc*, *no_downstream_ejc*, *truncated_no_ejc*), re-intersected on `(gene_id, reference_isoform_id)` after the category filter so the NMD and Control arms share the same gene-reference universe (same scope as Figure 4 Panels C/D). At this ~6× larger cohort the comparator's CDS comes mostly from novel transcripts (TD2-derived), so all three measures are anchored on the **ref-AUG-projected stop** rather than the comparator's own annotated stop — this avoids TD2's PTC-avoidance bias (Figure 4 / §4). **(D)** CDS length (ref-AUG-projected ORF). NMD+/PTC+ has the shortest ORF (median 579 nt vs Control 972 nt) — the ref-AUG is truncated by the PTC. NMD+/PTC− sits between (median 686 nt). **(E)** 3′UTR length, **translation-based** (tx_length − ref-AUG-projected stop). PTC+ inflation is again apparent (median 2,018 nt vs Control 1,184 nt); the same artifact as Panel B, now at the larger scope. **(F)** 3′UTR length, **non-PTC-stop based** (walks past PTCs). After bias correction, PTC+ moves from 2,018 down to 1,290 nt — closer to Control's 948 nt — and the directional pattern of the Section A row is preserved at the larger scope.

Violins show per-group density (`cut = 0`); horizontal lines mark q25 (dotted), median (dashed), and q75 (dotted) on a log₁₀ scale. Stars: ***p < 10⁻⁴, **p < 10⁻³, *p < 0.05, n.s. otherwise (two-sided Wilcoxon rank-sum).

**Cross-row interpretation.** The bias-corrected 3′UTR pattern (Panels C / F) replicates across the two scopes. At the smaller GENCODE-only scope (C) the PTC+/Control 3′UTR distinction collapses entirely (p = 0.90); at the larger ref-AUG-traceable scope (F) the directional pattern is the same and statistically detectable, but quantitatively smaller than Panel E's translation-based gap. This argues against extended 3′UTR length as the dominant NMD mechanism for the residual NMD+/PTC− pairs.

**Abbreviations.** NMD, nonsense-mediated decay; PTC, premature termination codon; CDS, coding sequence; UTR, untranslated region; AUG, methionine start codon; EJC, exon–exon junction complex; ENST, GENCODE/Ensembl transcript identifier prefix.
