**Supplemental Figure | Descriptive characterisation of the gene-matched isoform pair cohort.**

Three descriptive panels for the 3,009-gene cohort used in the pair analyses. For each gene we selected the highest-expressed non-NMD isoform as the *reference* and built two pairs: one against the most NMD-responsive isoform of the same gene (the NMD pair) and one against the next-highest-expressed non-NMD isoform of the same gene (the Control pair). Each gene therefore contributes one matched triplet (reference, NMD comparator, Control comparator), yielding 3,009 NMD and 3,009 Control pairs total.

**(A)** Distribution of expressed non-NMD isoforms per gene across the 3,009 genes. Isoforms counted are those passing expression filtering and not classified as NMD-sensitive. Median = 7 isoforms per gene (IQR = 5).

**(B)** Reference-isoform expression as a fraction of the parent gene's total non-NMD expression. Expression is the per-isoform mean across vehicle-treatment samples in the four manuscript cell types (alveolar type 2, large-airway epithelial, fibroblast, microvascular endothelial); the gene total is the sum across all non-NMD isoforms of the gene (same set as Panel A). Median reference share = 31%; 38% of references are above the 50% threshold (orange dotted line).

**(C)** Transcript-length distribution by role within each pair. Each gene contributes one matched triplet, so all three groups have n = 3,009. Lengths are spliced transcript lengths. Median lengths: NMD comparator 3,049 nt, reference 2,893 nt, Control comparator 2,762 nt. Pairwise paired-Wilcoxon comparisons across the gene-matched triplets: NMD vs reference p = 1.5×10⁻⁴; NMD vs Control p = 1.7×10⁻¹²; Control vs reference p = 3.8×10⁻⁵. Violins show kernel-density estimates on a log scale; the solid bar marks the median, dotted bars q25 and q75. Stars: ****p < 10⁻¹⁰, ***p < 10⁻⁴, **p < 10⁻³, *p < 0.05, n.s. otherwise.

**Abbreviations.** NMD, nonsense-mediated decay; IQR, interquartile range.
