**SF27 | Transcript Length by Pair Role.** Violin distribution of transcript length (log10 nt) for the three roles within each of the 3,009 gene-matched isoform triplets: NMD comparator (orange), Reference (grey), Control comparator (blue). Reference is the highest-expressed non-NMD isoform per gene under DMSO across the four cell types (AT2, LAE, FB, MV); the NMD and Control comparators are the paired NMD-susceptible and non-NMD non-reference isoforms from the same gene.
