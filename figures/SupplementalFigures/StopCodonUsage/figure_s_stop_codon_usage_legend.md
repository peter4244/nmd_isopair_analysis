**Supplemental Figure | NMD-susceptible isoforms preferentially use UGA over UAA as their stop codon.**

Within-class frequency of the three canonical stop codons (UGA / UAA / UAG) at the main open reading frame of each transcript in the deep-learning model's held-out test cohort (n = 2,268 NMD-susceptible isoforms, 7,863 non-NMD controls). UGA is enriched in NMD-susceptible isoforms relative to non-NMD controls (55.9% vs 48.1%); UAA is depleted (23.9% vs 29.9%); UAG is essentially unchanged with a small depletion (20.2% vs 22.0%). The direction is concordant with the per-nucleotide signed attribution at the stop codon in the model (Figure 5 Panel F), where G at the variable second position carries positive signed contribution — consistent with the model treating UGA-defining nucleotides as a positive predictor of NMD susceptibility.

**Abbreviations.** NMD, nonsense-mediated decay; ORF, open reading frame; UGA / UAA / UAG, the three canonical stop codons in RNA notation.
