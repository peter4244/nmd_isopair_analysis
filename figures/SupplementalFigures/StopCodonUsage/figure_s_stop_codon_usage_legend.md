**Supplemental Figure | NMD-susceptible isoforms preferentially use UGA over UAA as their stop codon.**

Within-class frequency of the three canonical stop codons (UGA / UAA / UAG) at the main ORF of each transcript in the deep-learning model's held-out test cohort. UGA is enriched in NMD-susceptible isoforms relative to non-NMD controls (55.9% vs 48.1%); UAA and UAG are both depleted in NMD. The direction is concordant with the per-nucleotide signed attribution at the stop codon in the model (Figure 5 Panel F), where G at the variable second position carries positive signed contribution — consistent with the model treating UGA-defining nucleotides as a positive predictor of NMD susceptibility.

**Abbreviations.** NMD, nonsense-mediated decay; ORF, open reading frame; UGA / UAA / UAG, the three canonical stop codons in RNA notation.
