**Supplemental Figure | NMD-susceptible isoforms preferentially use UGA over UAA as their stop codon.**

Within-class frequency of the three canonical stop codons (UGA / UAA / UAG) among rank-0 ORFs of the deep-learning model's chr-1/3/5/7 paralog-free test set (full test set; n_test = 10,131; ~2,268 NMD + ~7,863 Control). UGA is enriched in NMD (55.9% vs 48.1% in Control); UAA and UAG are both depleted in NMD. The direction agrees with the per-position SHAP attribution at the stop codon (Figure 5 Panel F), where G at the variable second position carries positive signed contribution — consistent with the model treating UGA-defining nucleotides as a positive feature for NMD prediction. **DRAFT — pending cluster re-render to populate exact UAA / UAG percentages from the post-patch `selected_orfs.tsv` (the embedded PNG is from the pre-patch Apr 2 render of `orf_model_report_v5.html`; the bug patch `scripts/patch_stop_codon.py` is dated Apr 30).**

**Abbreviations.** NMD, nonsense-mediated decay; ORF, open reading frame; UGA / UAA / UAG, the three canonical stop codons (RNA notation; corresponding DNA-template codons TGA / TAA / TAG appear on the figure axis); SHAP, SHapley Additive exPlanation.
