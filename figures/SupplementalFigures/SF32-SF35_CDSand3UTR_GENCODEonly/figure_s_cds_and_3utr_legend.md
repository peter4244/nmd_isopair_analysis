**SF32 + SF35 | Coding-sequence length and two complementary 3′UTR-length measures, at two cohort scopes.**  Row 1 (n = 190 GENCODE-restricted) supports SF32; row 2 (n = 1,166 ref-AUG-traceable) supports SF35.

The figure presents the same three measures (coding-sequence length; 3′UTR length under a naïve translation-based measure; 3′UTR length under a bias-corrected measure that walks past any premature stop codon) at two complementary cohorts. The top row uses the strict annotated cohort in which all three transcripts of every triplet are annotated coding transcripts (n = 72 NMD with a premature termination codon; n = 118 NMD without; n = 190 Control; the cohort that supports Figure 3 Panels D–F and Figure 4 Panels A and B). The bottom row uses the broader cohort in which the reference transcript is annotated and the reference start codon can be projected onto each comparator (n = 1,050 NMD with a premature termination codon; n = 116 NMD without; n = 1,166 Control; the cohort that supports Figure 4 Panels C and D). Premature-termination-codon classification in the top row is taken from each comparator's own annotated stop codon under the canonical 50-nt rule; in the bottom row it is taken from the reference start codon projected onto the comparator transcript, which avoids the systematic premature-termination-codon-avoidance bias of the TransDecoder2 CDS caller (documented in SF33/SF34).

**(A)** Coding-sequence length is similar across the three groups in the annotated cohort (medians 760 / 834 / 968 nt). NMD-susceptible isoforms with a premature termination codon have a modestly shorter coding sequence than Controls (Hodges–Lehmann shift = −186 nt, p = 5×10⁻³), consistent with the premature termination codon truncating the open reading frame. Coding-sequence length is not a confound for the 5′UTR and 3′UTR differences shown in the other panels.

**(B)** 3′UTR length under the translation-based measure (transcript length minus annotated stop position). NMD-susceptible isoforms with a premature termination codon appear to have substantially longer 3′UTRs than Controls (medians 869 vs 517 nt; p = 1×10⁻⁴), but this is a measurement artifact: the post-stop region in these isoforms includes the coding sequence between the premature stop and the natural stop.

**(C)** 3′UTR length under the bias-corrected measure that walks past any premature stop codon (translates downstream from the annotated start, skipping stops that would themselves be classified as premature, until reaching the first non-premature stop). Under this corrected measure, NMD with a premature termination codon and Controls are indistinguishable (medians 524 vs 517 nt; p = 0.90), confirming that the apparent inflation in Panel B was the measurement, not the biology. NMD-susceptible isoforms without a premature termination codon retain a significantly shorter 3′UTR than Controls (median 384 vs 517 nt; p = 0.03). Six of the 72 NMD-with-PTC isoforms (8%) had no non-premature stop downstream in the comparator transcript and are dropped from this panel (n = 66 displayed).

**(D)** Coding-sequence length on the broader cohort, anchored on the reference start codon projected onto the comparator. NMD-susceptible isoforms with a premature termination codon have the shortest open reading frame (median 579 nt vs 972 nt in Control), as expected from the premature stop. NMD-susceptible isoforms without a premature termination codon sit between (median 686 nt).

**(E)** Translation-based 3′UTR length on the broader cohort. The same artifact apparent in Panel B is again present (median 2,018 nt in NMD+PTC vs 1,184 nt in Control).

**(F)** Bias-corrected 3′UTR length on the broader cohort. After correction, NMD-with-PTC moves from 2,018 down to 1,290 nt, much closer to the Control median of 948 nt. The directional pattern from the annotated cohort is preserved at the broader cohort and remains statistically detectable, but it is quantitatively much smaller than the translation-based gap. This argues against extended 3′UTR length as the dominant mechanism for the residual NMD-without-PTC isoforms.
